The glass header no longer doubles up on short pages.

## The pill at rest

At the top of a page the glass theme shows the floating pill with the site title, and the frosted bar fades in only as the page scrolls. A page too short to scroll used to draw both at once. It now rests on the pill alone, the same as any page at its top. Reduced motion, reduced transparency and more contrast keep the frosted bar in place.
