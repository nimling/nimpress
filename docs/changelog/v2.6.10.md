  title: One footer
  description: The page footer line and the nimpress branding share one footer, and the branding is hidden with hide branding.
---

Every page now has one footer. Its row is a three column grid: the Built with nimpress notice, muted, in the first cell, the footer line, copyright and social links centered in the middle cell, and whitespace in the last cell. The line still comes from the page's `footer` field or the site's `footer.text`.

The branding is hidden per page with `hide: [branding]`, or on every page through `defaultFrontmatter`. `footer.generator` is removed; move a `generator: false` to `hide: [branding]` in `defaultFrontmatter`.
