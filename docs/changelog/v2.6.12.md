  title: Glass that holds any brand
  description: The glass theme takes its neutrals and light from the brand color, and the color scheme follows the operating system until the reader picks one.
---

The glass theme no longer carries warm brown neutrals of its own. Its ink, lines, shadows and surfaces mix from `--np-brand`, so a violet or a blue brand gets glass that belongs to it. The field is lit by three blooms instead of one: the brand from the top left, its hue turned from the top right and turned back from below. Ground, cover and slip glass take a stronger frost, a light sheen and a bright top edge, and the grain is much finer.

At the top of a page the logo, title, sidebar toggle and breadcrumbs sit in one frosted pill, `np-header-lead`. As content scrolls under the header the pill fades out and the header frosts in over the same 96 pixels, all in CSS on the scroll timeline. The header's frost moved to a layer of its own so the pill can frost the page beneath it.

Sidebar rows are one line. A long title ends in an ellipsis inside `np-node-text` and shows whole in its tooltip. The active row is lit cover glass with a bright edge instead of a brand ring.

The light and dark scheme follows the operating system and changes with it live until the reader presses the toggle in the header. Only that choice is remembered, so a first visit no longer pins the scheme the system had at the time.
