  title: Variant pages follow the dropdown order
  description: The pages in a variant folder list in the order the dropdown declares its values, and the folder keeps its own place among its siblings.
---

The pages of a variant folder take an `order` from their value's position in the dropdown, so `Vue` lists before `Svelte` when the `dropdowns` config block declares `values: ['Vue', 'Svelte']`. In 2.7.0 the recursive alphabetical sort undid that order. A variant folder no longer takes the lowest order of its pages, so `Button` stays at its alphabetical place in its group.

The site title in the header stays on one line. When the header runs short of room, the title ends in an ellipsis inside `np-brand` and the logo keeps its size, instead of the title wrapping onto a second line inside the glass pill.
