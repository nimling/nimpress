  title: Gated pages open on first load
  description: A gated page now renders for a viewer who holds its gate, on a direct load and on the return from login.
---

A page with `gate` in its frontmatter used to fall back to the home page even when the viewer held the gate, because the router learned its routes before the gated pages had arrived. The site now waits for the viewer and the gated pages before it routes, showing the loading spinner meanwhile, so a direct link to a gated page and the return after login both open that page. A site without auth routes immediately as before.
