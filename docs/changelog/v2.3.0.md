A diagram looks like the rest of the site.

## Cards instead of boxes

Every table is a card with a colored header, a note under its name, one row per column, and its indexes in the footer. Keys, foreign keys, and unique columns carry their own small labels. Every color reads from the site theme, so a diagram follows a light or dark reader and any palette a site overrides.

## Lines between columns

A relationship enters and leaves the column it actually joins, on whichever side of the card faces the other table, and a to-many line carries an arrow head at the child end.
