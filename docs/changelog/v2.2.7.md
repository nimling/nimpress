Scrolling past a diagram scrolls the page.

## Click to explore

An inline diagram mounts inert behind a short invitation. Clicking it hands the frame the pointer and swaps the invitation for a hint naming the gestures. A full page diagram skips the wait, because there the diagram is the page.
