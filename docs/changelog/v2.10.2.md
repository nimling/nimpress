  title: Glass header on scroll in every browser
  description: The glass header shows its pill at the top of the page and frosts over as you scroll, in Firefox as well as in Chrome and Safari.
---

Browsers without scroll driven animations, such as Firefox and Zen, kept the glass header frosted from the top of the page and never showed the pill around the site name. The header now follows the scroll there too: the pill shows at the top, and the header frosts over once the page scrolls. Browsers with scroll driven animations keep the gradual fade they had, and reduced motion turns both off.
