Roadmaps with many features on one epic now read cleanly. Every card has its own space, the spine winds around them, and the rocket sits on the spine at today's date.

## Clusters around each epic

An epic and its features form one cluster. The epic faces the spine, and its features ring around it in date order, the earliest below and the latest above. A cluster with more features pushes the spine further across the page, so the timeline uses the whole width.

## Cards sized to fit

Cards are as large as the space allows and size down only when a cluster needs it. The smallest card shows the date and the title. Every card is an organic shape bordered in the color of its kind, with the kind as a badge on its top edge, and the shape wraps its text closely.

## Lines to every feature

Every feature has a soft curved line to its parent. The spine never runs through a card, curves smoothly into every issue, and fills the open space between clusters, with more room for a longer gap in time.

## Hover and travel

The hover card fits its content and fades out at the bottom with a click to see more when the issue runs long. Travel runs slower, the page follows the rocket, and the rocket and today marker sit at the right place between the issues around today.
