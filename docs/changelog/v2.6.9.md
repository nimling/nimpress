  title: Logout removes gated content
  description: Logging out removes every gated page from memory and routes its address to gateDenied without a reload.
---

Logging out now removes every gated page the session loaded, with its route, its search entries and the gated sidebar, and rebuilds the routes, so a gated address follows `gateDenied` at once without a reload. Gated content is never written to browser storage. Pair it with a provider that answers guarded bundles with `Cache-Control: no-store` and its logout with `Clear-Site-Data: "cache"`, as samna_auth does, and nothing gated outlives the session in the browser.
