  title: Gated pages redirect a denied viewer
  description: A viewer the gate denies is sent to the page's gateRedirect, the site's auth.gateRedirect, or the site root.
---

A gated page a viewer cannot load redirects the same way a page `redirect` does: to the path in its `gateRedirect` front matter, else to `auth.gateRedirect` in the config, else to `/`. The targets travel in the public `access.json`, so the redirect works without the guarded bundle.

```yaml
gate: internal
gateRedirect: /pricing
```
