Travel now feels like a launch.

## Flame and trail

While the rocket travels, a flame burns behind it with a white hot core, a warm body, and a glow that licks and flickers like real exhaust. The flame trails a little behind the rocket's heading, so it swings out through every turn and settles as the path straightens. A glowing trail follows the exact curve the rocket just flew.

## A rocket that stands out

The rocket has a silver hull with colored fins and a blue window, so it reads clearly against the path and the flame, and it shakes slightly as it flies. Reduced motion keeps the rocket still and the flame calm.
