  title: gateDenied for signed out viewers
  description: A signed out viewer on a gated route follows gateDenied like a signed in viewer without the gate.

`gateDenied` now applies to a signed out viewer as well. The runtime reads the gated routes from `access.json` for every viewer on a site with auth configured, and fetches guarded bundles only for a signed in viewer.
