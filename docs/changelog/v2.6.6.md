  title: Choose what a denied gated page does
  description: gateDenied, in front matter or as auth.gateDenied, redirects a denied viewer to a path or shows the not found page.
---

A gated page a viewer cannot load follows `gateDenied`: a path redirects there the same way a page `redirect` does, and `404` shows the not found page and keeps the address. The page's own value wins over `auth.gateDenied` in the config, and with neither the viewer is redirected to `/`. `gateDenied` replaces `gateRedirect` from 2.6.5.

```yaml
gate: internal
gateDenied: 404
```
