A schema is readable as a diagram instead of a wall of statements.

## Inline and as a page

A fenced `dbml` block renders a diagram inline in any page, and a page with `type: dbml` turns one `.dbml` file into a full viewer. The frame pans, zooms, and lets a reader drag a table aside.

## What it draws

Tables with their columns, types, defaults, and notes, primary and composite keys, unique and not null columns, named indexes, header colors, standalone notes as stickies, and every relationship as a line between the two columns it joins.

The schema is parsed while the site builds, so the page ships the diagram rather than the parser.
