Roadmaps open faster and offer a second, calmer way to read them.

## Straight view

A button beside Travel switches the roadmap to a straight view. The spine runs straight down the middle, each epic sits on alternating sides, and its features line up in tidy columns underneath with straight connecting lines. The cards keep their look with straighter edges. The choice is remembered for the next visit, and switching moves every card smoothly to its new place.

## Laid out ahead of time

The roadmap is laid out when the site is built, so the page no longer works it out in the browser. It scales to any window width as one picture, text included, and stays a single column on small screens.

## Revealed when ready

The timeline, its lines and the planet footer appear together once everything is in place, with the cards rising in one after another. A loading indicator shows while a large roadmap is still being prepared.
