  title: Header in Firefox
  description: The site logo and title keep their size in Firefox and other Gecko browsers.
---

In Firefox, Zen and other Gecko browsers the header logo grew to the natural size of the image and pushed the header actions down the page, and the site title in the glass theme was cut to an ellipsis. The header now holds the logo and the title at the same size in every browser. Browsers without scroll driven animations show the glass header frosted from the top instead of fading it in on scroll.
